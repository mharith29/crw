Custom & Bulk Orders

Bespoke CNC hardware for teams, shops, and distributors. We design and manufacture to your spec, validated to the same structural standard as everything else we build.

100Minimum Per Design

Custom and white-label runs start at 100 pieces per design. This lets us hold the same tolerances and finish quality on a custom order as we do on our own product line — smaller runs don't allow for that.

How It Works

01

Enquire

Tell us what you need built — application, quantity, and any reference drawings or parts.

02

Design Review

We assess feasibility, material, and tolerances, and confirm whether your run fits within the 100-unit minimum.

03

Quote & Sample

You receive pricing and lead time. A sample unit can be produced for approval before the full run.

04

Production

CNC machining, finishing, and structural validation, then delivery of your full order.

From CAD to Validated Part

Every custom design goes through the same engineering rigor as our own product line.

Design & Modeling

Software

Modeled in SOLIDWORKS, from reference geometry through to final tolerances.

Output

A production-ready CAD file, checked against your application before a single piece is cut.

Material Validation

Test Environment

Independent lab testing (Zwick/Roell UTM) comparing candidate materials under load.

Why It Matters

304 stainless steel handles roughly 2.5× the load of 6061 aluminium before deforming — informing which material we recommend for your part.
